Background & interests

Profile

I am a Ph.D. student in Control Science and Engineering at Southeast University, advised by Prof. Yangang Wang in the Vision and Cognition Lab.

My research focuses on 3D reconstruction and generation, with an emphasis on digital humans, dynamic garments, and human–object and human–scene interactions. I aim to recover and generate geometrically coherent, temporally consistent 3D content from images, videos, and language.

I received my bachelor’s degree in Automation from Southeast University in 2022, then pursued master’s-level studies in Control Science and Engineering before starting my Ph.D. in March 2024.
